No analytics, advertising or accounts

Online Wallpaper uses no analytics or advertising services, tracking, or user accounts. The app does not send data to a bury.link server.

Local storage

Your image URL, refresh preferences, image framing and image position are stored only on your device. The app also keeps a small local cached preview of the last downloaded image.

Image downloads

When you manually or automatically update the wallpaper, the app requests the image URL you selected directly. The selected image server receives the connection information technically needed for a normal image download, including the requested URL, your IP address, the time of request and technical HTTP request data. That server's privacy policy applies to its processing.

HTTPS connections are encrypted. If you choose an HTTP URL, for example for a source on your local network, the transfer is not encrypted.

Permissions

The app uses internet access to download the image, network status for scheduled updates, and permission to set the lock-screen wallpaper. It can also open Android's battery settings only when you choose that action.
