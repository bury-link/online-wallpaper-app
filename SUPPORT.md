# Support & donations

The Play Store build of **Online Wallpaper** deliberately does **not** show a donation button or open a payment provider from inside the app.

Google Play's Payments policy prohibits Play-distributed apps from leading users to payment methods other than Google Play Billing except for limited, specific exemptions. The app has no paid feature, upgrade, digital good, or entitlement tied to support, but a commercial PayPal/Ko-fi contribution is not clearly within the policy's explicit **tax-exempt donation** exception. Keeping these links out of the Play build avoids an avoidable review risk.

## Official support links

Use these links on the project website, in social profiles, or in non-Play distribution materials:

- PayPal: https://paypal.me/burylink
- Ko-fi: https://ko-fi.com/burylink

## Play Store guidance

- Do not put these URLs in the app UI, Play listing, in-app WebViews, or a help page reachable from the Play build.
- Do not offer any app feature, content, priority, or other benefit in return for support.
- If the business later becomes eligible to collect **tax-exempt donations**, re-check the current Play Payments policy and tax status before adding an in-app flow.

Policy reference checked 2026-08-25: https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9858738?hl=en
